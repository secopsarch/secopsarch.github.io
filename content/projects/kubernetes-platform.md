## Goal

Explore a dependable baseline for teams running container workloads on Kubernetes: clear workload boundaries, repeatable environment setup and an operational model that supports day-two work.

## Areas of focus

- Cluster lifecycle and workload placement
- Configuration, secrets and service connectivity
- Observability and troubleshooting workflows
- Secure-by-default workload conventions

## Outcome

A practical platform foundation that can be iterated on as application, security and operational needs evolve.
