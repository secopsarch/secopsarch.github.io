## Goal

Develop and document repeatable operating practices for an OpenShift environment, with attention to the components that have the greatest impact on workload reliability.

## Areas of focus

- Operator lifecycle and platform capabilities
- Storage, networking and monitoring dependencies
- Workload troubleshooting and capacity awareness
- Runbook-oriented operational documentation

## Outcome

A structured foundation for operating the platform consistently while reducing time-to-diagnosis for common issues.
